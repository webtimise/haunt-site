Last updated 9 November 2026

Ask for deletion

  1. Email ybenammou@gmail.com with the subject “Delete my Haunt data”.
  2. Include your Haunt username, or the phone number you signed in with.
  3. We confirm by email and delete your account and data within 30 days.

What gets deleted

Your account, profile, saves, opinions, notes, lists you own, comments, reactions, follows, blocks, uploaded photos, import history, contact hashes and push tokens.

Lists you collaborated on keep the places other people added, but your additions and your membership are removed.

Instagram and Facebook data

Haunt doesn't use Facebook Login and stores no Facebook or Instagram account data. When you import a post, only the post's public caption is read to find places, and the links you imported are deleted along with your account.
