Privacy policy

Last updated 9 November 2026

Haunt is an iOS app for saving and sharing places in Paris. This policy explains what we collect, why, who processes it and the choices you have. Haunt is operated by Youness Benammou (“we”). Contact: ybenammou@gmail.com.

What we collect

DataWhy
Account: an anonymous account ID, and your phone number if you sign in with itTo keep your saves and lists on your account
Profile: name, username, bio, photo, home city, Instagram/TikTok handles, interestsShown to other Haunt users you interact with
Saves: places you want to try or visited, your opinion and notesYour map, lists, profile stats and your friends' feed
Lists, collaborators, follows, blocks, comments and reactionsThe social features of the app
Imports: links you share or paste, captions and text you pasteTo find the places they mention
Contacts (only if you allow it): SHA-256 hashes of your contacts' phone numbers, never names or raw numbersTo show which of your contacts already use Haunt
Photos you choose to uploadShown on your posts
Push notification tokenTo send you notifications you turned on

Your location is used on your device to show where you are on the map. We don't store your location history. To load places, the app sends us the map area on your screen.

Who processes it

  • Supabase: database, authentication, file storage and server functions, hosted in the EU (Ireland).
  • Mapbox: map display. Mapbox receives map tile requests and your IP address.
  • Meta (Instagram oEmbed) and TikTok (oEmbed): when you import an Instagram or TikTok link, its URL is sent to them to read the post's caption. Nothing else about you is sent.
  • Apple: push notifications.

Place information comes from Overture Maps Foundation (CDLA-Permissive-2.0).

We don't sell your data, show ads, or share your data with advertisers.

Your choices and rights

  • You can edit or delete your saves, lists, notes and profile in the app at any time.
  • You can turn off contacts, photos, location and notifications in iOS Settings.
  • You can ask for a copy of your data or for its deletion. See Delete your data.
  • If you are in the EU/EEA or UK, you have rights under the GDPR, including access, correction, deletion, restriction, portability and objection, and you can complain to your data protection authority (in France, the CNIL).

Retention

We keep your data while your account exists. When you delete your account, your data is removed within 30 days, except where the law requires us to keep it.

Children

Haunt is not intended for children under 13.

Changes

We'll update this page when the policy changes and note the date at the top.
